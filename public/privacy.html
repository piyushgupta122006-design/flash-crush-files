Privacy Policy

Flash Crush-Files Last updated: March 2026

1. No Data Collection

Flash Crush-Files does not collect, store, or transmit any of your files. All PDF and image compression happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device.

2. Google Sign-In

We use Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication) solely to identify you. We store your name, email address, and profile photo in Firebase for authentication purposes only. We do not sell or share this information with any third party.

3. Google Drive Access

If you choose to use the Google Drive feature, Flash Crush-Files requests access to your Drive files only to:

  • Let you pick a file from your Drive to compress or convert
  • Save a compressed or converted file back to your Drive

We only access files you explicitly select. We do not read, store, or process any other Drive content.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to measure basic usage (page views, feature usage). No personal data is included in analytics. No third-party advertising cookies are used.

5. Contact

Questions? Contact us through our official support channel.
